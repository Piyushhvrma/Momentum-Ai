# 🚀 StudyBuddy

> **An AI-powered collaborative learning platform that helps students stay organized, learn consistently, and study together in real time.**

StudyBuddy is a **full-stack MERN application** designed to bring everything a student needs into one workspace. From tracking daily study goals and organizing notes to collaborating through realtime study rooms with shared whiteboards and AI assistance, the platform focuses on improving productivity and collaborative learning.

# 💡 Why StudyBuddy?

Students often use multiple applications for notes, resources, discussions, AI tools, and progress tracking.

StudyBuddy brings these essential learning features together into **one unified platform**, reducing context switching while encouraging consistent and collaborative learning.
